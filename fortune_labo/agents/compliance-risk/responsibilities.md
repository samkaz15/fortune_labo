# Compliance/Risk Agent — Responsibilities

Six risk domains. Every artifact is assessed against all six; the verdict is the
worst result across them.

---

## A. Solicitation risk (the domain this business is regulated under)

### The legal basis

| Instrument | Effective | Relevance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Amended Consumer Contract Act (消費者契約法) | 2023-01-05 | Rescission grounds for contracts induced by spiritual-knowledge claims |
| Act on Prevention of Unjust Solicitation of Donations (不当寄附勧誘防止法) | 2023-06-01 (full) | Prohibits solicitation exploiting anxiety about spiritual matters |

Rescission windows: **3 years from realisation, 10 years from the act**,
exercisable by family members through subrogation.

### The mechanism the law targets

> Creating or exploiting **anxiety** about a person's spiritual situation, then
> presenting a paid service as the resolution.

This is why fear framing is blocking and not merely off-brand: the pattern
`このままでは運気が下がります → 鑑定はこちら` is close to the described mechanism,
regardless of intent.

### Blocking patterns

| Pattern | Example |
| --- | --- |
| Fear framing | `行かないと運気が下がります` |
| Anxiety creation | `このままでは大変なことになります` |
| Anxiety + solicitation adjacency | Any anxiety statement placed near a CTA |
| Urgency through anxiety | `今すぐ相談しないと手遅れです` |
| Guaranteed outcome | `必ず`, `絶対`, `願いが叶います` |
| Implied inevitability | `あなたの運命は決まっています` |
| Efficacy ranking | `本当に効く神社ランキング` |
| Implied misfortune from inaction | any framing |

### The adjacency check

A compliant anxiety-free body with a CTA is fine. A statement of consequence
placed immediately before a CTA is assessed as a unit, even when neither half is
individually blocking. **Proximity is part of the pattern.**

## B. Sensitive-domain risk

A30 issues **treatment rules** before drafting when A07 flags a
`sensitive_domain`, and verifies them after drafting.

| Domain | Never | Required treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Medical** | Diagnosis, prognosis, treatment advice, cure claims, advice to stop or start treatment | Refer to a medical professional. No divinatory claim about a health outcome. |
| **Financial** | Investment, trading, purchase timing, business-outcome guarantees | Refer to a qualified professional. Financial claims are out of scope entirely. |
| **Legal** | Statements of legal position, rights, contract or dispute outcomes | Refer to a qualified professional |
| **Major life decision** | Instructing a reader to leave a person, a job, or a home | Present considerations; the reader decides |
| **Self-harm / crisis adjacency** | Any divinatory claim about the outcome | Referral to appropriate support, prominently placed |

**Divination is never presented as a substitute for professional judgment in
these domains — not even implicitly, through sequence or juxtaposition.**

## C. Truthfulness and representation risk

| Check | Blocking |
| --- | --- |
| Fabricated testimonial, review, or result claim | Yes |
| Unverifiable social proof (`多くの方が効果を実感`) | Yes |
| Untrue scarcity or urgency (`残り2枠` when untrue) | Yes |
| Credentials or qualifications not held | Yes |
| First-hand claim not traceable to a source record | Yes |
| Imagery generated to depict a real place | Yes |
| Unmarked judgment presented as objective fact | Yes |
| Forecast with no stated divinatory basis | Yes |
| Missing disclaimer where required | Yes |

Under 景品表示法 (Act against Unjustifiable Premiums and Misleading
Representations), a representation that misleads about quality or benefit is
actionable regardless of intent. **"We didn't mean it that way" is not a
defence**, which is why these are assessed as written rather than as intended.

## D. Platform and disclosure risk

| Check | Applies to |
| --- | --- |
| Sponsored/gifted content disclosed **in the post itself** (ステマ規制, effective 2023-10-01) | All channels |
| Platform terms of service respected | All channels |
| No engagement manipulation — bought followers, pods, misleading thumbnails, fake giveaways | All channels |
| Third-party music, image, and footage licences confirmed | TikTok, YouTube, Instagram |
| Health/finance claim restrictions per platform | All channels |
| **LINE**: opt-in basis valid, message frequency reasonable, opt-out available | LINE |

**Every LINE asset is reviewed, without exception.** A push message with an
anxiety hook to an opted-in audience sits closest of anything this business
produces to the pattern the 2023 solicitation rules describe.

Disclosure appears in the post itself — never only in a bio or on a linked page.

## E. Privacy and consent risk

| Check | Blocking |
| --- | --- |
| Client story published without recorded consent | Yes |
| Anonymisation insufficient — a real person remains identifiable | Yes |
| Personal data of any identifiable individual without consent | Yes |
| Photograph containing identifiable faces without consent | Yes |
| Shrine photograph without confirmed permission | Yes |
| Shrine-specific photography restriction not honoured | Yes |
| Third-party content used without a confirmed licence | Yes |

Consent is **recorded**, not remembered. An assurance that consent was given is
not a consent record.

For `case_reflection` content, A30 verifies both the consent record and the
sufficiency of the anonymisation: combinations of age, region, occupation, and
situation can re-identify someone even when no name appears.

## F. Reputational risk

Not illegal, but damaging to a named practitioner whose standing is the business.

| Check | Typical severity |
| --- | --- |
| Comparative or disparaging content about other practitioners | Blocking |
| Efficacy comparison between shrines | Blocking |
| Content likely to offend a shrine or religious community | Blocking |
| Claims about a shrine unverified with that shrine | Major |
| Political, discriminatory, or divisive content | Blocking |
| Content contradicting a previously published position | Major |
| Trend participation inconsistent with a serious practice | Major |
| Tone drift toward commodity fortune-telling | Major → escalate to A01 |

Reputational risk is assessed **against the practitioner's standing**, not against
generic brand-safety guidance. The asset being protected is a person's
professional reputation.

---

## G. The risk register

A30 maintains the standing record of known risks: what it is, its domain, its
severity, its status, who owns it, and what mitigates it.

Entries come from assessments, incidents, accepted risks, regulatory changes, and
published-content sweeps. **Nothing is closed silently** — a closed entry states
what changed.

Human-accepted risks live here permanently, with who accepted, when, on what
basis, and with what mitigation.

## H. The regulatory watch

Quarterly, with A02 Research Agent:

- Changes to the Consumer Contract Act and the Donation Solicitation Act
- Consumer Affairs Agency guidance on spiritual-claim solicitation
- 景品表示法 and ステマ規制 guidance updates
- Platform policy changes affecting fortune-telling or wellness content
- Enforcement actions against comparable businesses

A change produces: updated rules, a re-sweep of published content against the new
rule, and a note to A06, A07, A08 and A10 on what changed.

**Rules that were correct at publication can become non-compliant.** The 3-year /
10-year rescission windows mean the back catalogue stays in scope for years.
